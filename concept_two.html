The Celluloid Revolt against Commercial Formula

Marathe Motion Pictures was founded in a former cotton gin warehouse in Goregaon. Disillusioned with artificial song-and-dance formula cinema of Bombay, Anand Rao Marathe pawned his family ancestral estate in Satara to import a single Mitchell NC 35mm camera, insisting on naturalistic lighting, location sound recording, and stories drawn from rural Marathi literature.

The Masterwork of 1994

Kaalchakra: The Wheel of Time

When Indian cinema shifted toward synthetic synthesizers in the late eighties, Marathe Studios doubled down on acoustic purism. The 1994 magnum opus Kaalchakra blended 42 Indian classical instruments with a full Prague string section, recorded directly to multi-track magnetic reels under legendary conductor Pandit Hariprasad Chaurasia.

Dynasty & Authorship The Three Generations of Marathe Auteurs
1974 — 2026 Pedigree
Generation I 1974–2004

Anand Rao Marathe

The Patriarch & Pioneer

Founder and foundational visionary. Directed 22 landmark social dramas. Conferred the Dadasaheb Phalke Lifetime Achievement Award in 1999 for introducing realist poetics to commercial Marathi and Hindi cinema.

• Landmark Films: Surya Jyoti, Asha Kiran, Sahyadri • Key Camera: Mitchell NC 35mm
Generation II 2000–2020

Vikramaditya Marathe

The Architect of Scale

Expanded Marathe Studios into global co-productions with France, Japan, and the UK. Built the Film City soundstages and inaugurated Project Sanrakshan film preservation vault. Two-time National Film Award laureate.

• Landmark Films: Kaalchakra, Raajmudra, Monsoon Horizon • Innovation: 6-Track Dolby Stereo Lab
Generation III 2021–Present

Aryan Marathe

The Heir & Visionary

FTII Pune & USC Cinematic Arts alumnus. Combining five decades of family analog heritage with anamorphic 65mm optics and immersive multi-sensory cinema. Writer-Director of the 2026 epic DHANANJAY.

• Upcoming Milestone: DHANANJAY: The Heir (Nov 2026) • Format: 65mm Photochemical + IMAX 70mm
