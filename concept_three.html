Golden Jubilee Retrospective

1974 – 2024: Fifty Years of Celluloid Excellence

Founded in the nascent cinematic boom of 1974 by Dadasaheb Phalke laureate Anand Rao Marathe, Marathe Motion Pictures established the gold standard for dramatic storytelling in Indian cinema. Over five continuous decades, the studio championed bold auteur voices, transition from 35mm black-and-white realism to sweeping anamorphic historicals.

With 68 landmark features, 14 National Film Awards, and 7 Cannes selections, our archives safeguard original master reels, hand-painted vintage lobby cards, and the architectural history of Indian screenwriting.

The Third Generation Takes The Helm

Rooted in Legacy. Driven by Modern Cinematic Scale.

"Cinema is not merely the images we cast upon silver linen; it is the pulse of memory passed down through blood and craft."

Trained under veteran masters at the Film and Television Institute of India (FTII) and USC's School of Cinematic Arts, Aryan Marathe served twelve rigorous years across production design, screenwriting, and second-unit direction. With DHANANJAY, Aryan brings an unapologetically grand, grounded historical epic that honours the visual language established by his grandfather Anand Rao in 1974.
