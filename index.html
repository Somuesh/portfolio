Generational Continuity

Inheriting the Sacred Hearth

“Cinema in our family was never merely an industry; it was a sacred hearth where myth, human frailty, and technical bravery met. Stepping behind the camera is not about imitating our grandfather’s canvas—it is about honoring the fire that forged it while unfastening tomorrow.”

Growing up amidst 35mm film cans, orchestra rehearsal rooms in Goregaon, and scripts penned during pre-dawn hours, Kabir Sagar observed the delicate balance between epic scale and profound psychological intimacy. Having trained at USC Cinematic Arts followed by seven years assisting international masters, his debut feature “Aetbaar” represents both a continuation and a radical reinvention.

Under his creative direction, Sagar Films is expanding its preservation wing, restoring 18 heritage negatives in 4K Dolby Vision while simultaneously funding original scripts by emerging South Asian auteurs.
